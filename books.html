National Archive Hunters #1 Ages 8-12 Mystery & Adventure

Capitol Chase

In this high-stakes series starter, twins Ike and Iris Carter—a history genius and an elite athlete, respectively—stumble upon a robbery at their mother's boutique museum in Washington, D.C. They discover a string of thefts targeting artifacts once gifted by the Marquis de Lafayette. As they race to track down the thief and clear their family's name, they find themselves pursued by the FBI's Art Crime team, who wrongly suspect the twins' involvement.

National Archive Hunters #2 Ages 8-12 Mystery & Adventure

Eternal Flame

Now acting as unofficial consultants for the FBI's Art Crimes Team, twins Ike and Iris Carter are called back to action. Pieces of the Statue of Liberty's original flame, packed away since 1916, have mysteriously vanished right before a high-profile Smithsonian gala. The chase leads them from Washington, D.C., to Paris and New York, following the blueprints of Gustave Eiffel to recover the missing pieces before international relations sour.

Middle-Grade Ages 10-14 Contemporary Realism

Operation Final Notice

Ronny's father was injured at work, leaving the family in deep financial trouble. When Ronny finds an unpaid car bill marked "FINAL NOTICE", he launches a secret plan to make $878 to save their family car from repossession. Meanwhile, his best friend Jo is a talented cellist who is terrified of performing solo. As Jo prepares for an audition at a prestigious music academy, the two best friends learn to rely on their community and each other to face their anxieties.

Middle-Grade Ages 10-14 Historical / Contemporary

The Not-So-Boring Letters of Private Nobody

Seventh-grader Oliver Pritchard is a massive Civil War history fan. He gets paired with Ella Berry—a girl he thinks is a slacker—to research a local soldier, Private Raymond Stone, who died of disease without ever seeing a battle. Oliver thinks the project will be boring, but as they dig into the letters, Oliver and Ella uncover a massive secret in Stone's military record, finding an unexpected connection to the past and an unlikely friendship.

Middle-Grade Ages 10-14 Humor & Heart

It's the End of the World as I Know It

Thirteen-year-old Derrick is convinced that a doomsday event is coming. Ever since his mother died serving in Iraq, Derrick prepares for the worst by hoarding food and building an elaborate fallout shelter in his backyard. But when a new friend, Misty, challenges his prepper lifestyle, and a real, local disaster hits, Derrick has to leave his bunker, discovering that dealing with the end of the world is less about canned goods and more about opening up to people.

Young Adult Ages 12+ Thriller & Mystery

The League of American Traitors

Seventeen-year-old Jasper discovers at his father's funeral that he is the last living descendant of Benedict Arnold, the most famous traitor in American history. He is swept into a secret boarding school for descendants of historic traitors, discovering that his father's death was murder, and that he is caught in a multi-generational blood feud between the descendants of revolutionary patriots and traitors. Jasper has to clear Arnold's name before he turns 18, or pay the ultimate price.
