Student-Centered History

American Made

Hosted by Matt Landis

An American history podcast that explores the stories, questions, and struggles that built a nation. Positioned for teachers, parents, and homeschool co-ops (grades 3–8), each episode features an accessible, story-driven narrative complete with editable handouts, primary sources, and interactive Blooket games.
