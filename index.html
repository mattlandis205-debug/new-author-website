Interactive School Visits

Matthew is a full-time middle school history teacher who regularly visits schools and libraries across the country to speak with students. He offers high-energy assemblies, collaborative scavenger hunts, and workshops designed to show kids that reading, writing, and history are anything but boring.

  • High-energy "Big Assembly" sessions with live history stunts and prizes
  • Collaborative "Live Scavenger Hunt" team puzzle challenges
  • Classroom "Writers' Workshops" teaching practical storytelling skills
