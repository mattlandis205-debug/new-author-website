What happens at a school visit?

Big Assembly

The Big Assembly

Duration: 40–50 minutes

A high-energy, fast-paced session for combined grades. Matthew shares his writing journey, discusses the necessity of failure, and pulls students onstage for live history stunts, puzzles, and brain challenges based on the National Archive Hunters series. Prizes include signed books and an American flag cape!

Interactive Game

Live Scavenger Hunt

Duration: 40–50 minutes

Perfect for groups of up to 150 students. Working in small team tables, students solve a series of historical puzzles, map challenges, and book trivia directly tied to National Archive Hunters: Eternal Flame. High-energy, collaborative, and fast-paced.

Classroom Workshop

Writers' Workshops

Duration: 40–50 minutes

Designed for individual classroom sizes. Matthew works directly with students on specific writing skills, teaching them actionable techniques to "Write Not-Boring Stories." Students practice building compelling hooks, writing realistic dialogue, and editing.
